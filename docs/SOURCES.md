
# NikshayNavigator – Data Sources

## 1. Overview

NikshayNavigator uses official public information published by
the Central TB Division (CTD), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare,
Government of India.

The purpose is to provide guideline-based answers to healthcare
field staff with source attribution and guideline version tracking.

Date of initial source review: 21 November 2026.

## 3. Initial Document Collection

The initial document collection will prioritize:

1. National Guidelines for Management of DR-TB – 2025.
2. National Guidance on Differentiated TB Care – March 2025.
3. Guidelines for Programmatic Management of Tuberculosis Preventive Treatment in India.
4. Guidelines for Programmatic Management of Drug Resistant Tuberculosis in India – 2021.
5. Frequently Asked Questions on TB Preventive Treatment.
6. Relevant NTEP training modules and technical guidance.

The 2021 DR-TB document will be retained for historical comparison.
Its recommendations must not override newer applicable guidance.

## 4. File Formats

The source collection may contain:

- HTML: official source and category pages.
- PDF: guidelines, circulars and technical documents.
- Scanned PDF: documents requiring OCR, if encountered.
- Other linked formats: to be identified and recorded individually.

Each downloaded document must be inspected to determine whether
its text is searchable or requires OCR.

## 5. Copyright and Terms of Use

The CTD Copyright Policy states that website content must not be
reproduced partially or fully without prominently acknowledging
the source.

Third-party copyrighted material is excluded from the website's
general reproduction permission. Authorization must be obtained
from the relevant department or copyright holder.

The CTD Terms & Conditions describe website information as
reference material and advise users to verify ambiguous information
with the relevant department.

Project handling rules:

- Preserve the original document title and issuing authority.
- Record the publication year and source URL.
- Attribute every extracted document.
- Do not assume that publicly accessible PDFs have an unrestricted
  redistribution licence.
- Check document-specific copyright notices before redistribution.
- External links must be reviewed under their respective owners' policies.

## 6. Document Metadata

Each document record should preserve:

- id
- external_id
- title
- year
- topic
- source_url
- published_on
- document type
- applicable population
- supersedes information

Missing metadata must remain null until verified.
Do not invent publication dates or supersession relationships.

## 7. Version Management

NikshayNavigator must:

- Identify the guideline year used for every answer.
- Prefer the latest valid applicable guidance.
- Preserve older documents for historical comparison.
- Identify conflicts between document versions.
- Never combine incompatible recommendations from different versions.
