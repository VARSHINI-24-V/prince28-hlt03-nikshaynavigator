# NikshayNavigator – Evaluation Questions

These questions will be used to evaluate whether NikshayNavigator provides accurate, relevant and guideline-cited answers to TB programme queries.

## Q1. Diagnosis

What diagnostic test does NTEP recommend first for a presumptive TB patient?

## Q2. TB Preventive Treatment

Who is eligible for TB preventive treatment under the national guidelines?

## Q3. Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana

What nutritional support does Ni-kshay Poshan Yojana provide to TB patients?

## Q4. Drug-Resistant TB

A private doctor in Tiruvallur has a patient with rifampicin-resistant TB who was treated for TB two years ago. What does the latest DR-TB guideline say about the regimen and the tests needed before starting treatment?

## Q5. Guideline Comparison

Compare what the 2021 and 2025 DR-TB guidelines say about shorter oral regimens. Identify what changed.

## Q6. Patient-Specific Information (Unanswerable)

Has patient Murugan taken today's prescribed TB medication dose?

**Expected behaviour:** The system must not invent patient-specific information. It should clearly state that it cannot verify medication adherence because no patient register or dose-taking record is connected to NikshayNavigator.

## Q7. Differentiated TB Care

What differentiated care recommendations are provided in the latest NTEP guidance?

## Q8. Reporting Rules

What are the reporting responsibilities under the NTEP guidelines for a private healthcare provider diagnosing a TB patient?

## Q9. Guideline Version Conflict

If an older DR-TB guideline recommends a different regimen from a newer valid guideline, which guidance should NikshayNavigator prioritize? How should it explain the difference?

## Q10. Source Citation

When answering a TB programme question, what source information must NikshayNavigator provide so that field staff can verify the recommendation?

---

## Evaluation Criteria

- Answers must be based on official NTEP or Central TB Division documents.
- Every answer must cite the guideline document name and year.
- Newer valid guidance must take priority over superseded guidance.
- Regimens from different guideline years must never be mixed.
- The system must clearly communicate when information is unavailable.
